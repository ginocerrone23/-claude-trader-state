# Playbook changelog

- 2026-11-11: initial playbook.
- 2026-11-11 (analyst): **Added a price-band preference ($5–$100; no stock entries above $200)** to Candidate sources. Evidence: the day's strongest movers (MU $812 +12%, AMD $553 +7%, SNDK $988 +14%, AVGO $345) cannot be bought as a whole share under the $200/position limit. With fewer than 2 shares, sizing to a $20 max loss doesn't work. The watchlist core now centers on $5–$100 names.
- 2026-11-11 (analyst): **Added a required journal format** (one entry per run, including skips with their levels). Evidence: `journal/2026-11-11.md` does not exist, so no run left a record today and the review could not grade any decisions.
- 2026-11-12 (analyst): **Removed the forced-trade fallback** ("From 10:00am CT, if nothing has been opened today, take the best score >= 4"). Gino decided on 2026-11-11: no forced trades, a no-trade day beats poor trades. Replaced it with an explicit "no fallback, no lowering the threshold" line. Neither of today's trades used the fallback (HOOD scored 6, LUNR 9).
- 2026-11-12 (analyst): **Added a room-to-resistance rule.** R:R is measured to the nearest overhead level. The +1 R:R point counts only if that level is >= 2R away. No buys within 0.5R below an unbroken HOD or double top; wait for a 5-min close above it. Evidence: LUNR was bought at 14.48, just under the 14.61 open-high double top. MFE was +0.14R and it stopped at -0.97R. HOOD's 86.70 target sat above Wed's 86.15 high. MFE was +0.52R and it stopped at -1.01R. Both trades lost ~1R without ever approaching their targets.
- 2026-11-12 (analyst): **Restricted puts to underlyings priced about $25 or less**, with |delta| >= 0.15 and premium <= $0.20. Evidence: the B-list puts (CRWV, AAOI, IREN, APLD, SMCI, RGTI, SOFI) were skipped in 6 runs as "≤ $20 premium = far-OTM lottery". The 11/12 close SOFI chain shows that a $20 budget buys only ~0.17-delta weekly puts even on a $15.6 stock.
- 2026-11-13 (analyst): **Added a no-chase rule:** no long entry more than 1R above its trigger. Re-entry only via a pullback setup with its own stop. Evidence: 2 of the 3 losers so far were chases. NU (11/13) was bought at 13.19, 0.79 = **2.3R above its 12.40 watchlist trigger** after a 2.5-hour run. MFE was +0.33R and it stopped at -1R (13.01) in midday chop. LUNR (11/12) was bought 0.38 = 1.15R above its 14.10 trigger, MFE +0.14R, -1R. In contrast, NU taken inside trigger + 1R (12.63 at the 9:00 run) would have hit its 13.10 target (+0.81R), and CHA at 13.89 (0.3R above its trigger) was +0.91R at 2:45. Only one change today. The score threshold, stops and targets are unchanged (samples still tiny).
