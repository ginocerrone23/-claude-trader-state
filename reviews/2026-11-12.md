## Playbook changes made (3)
1. **Removed the "best score ≥ 4 after 10:00 if nothing opened" fallback** (Gino's 11/11 decision: no forced trades).
2. **Room to resistance:** the R:R point counts only if the nearest overhead level is ≥ 2R away. No buys within 0.5R below an unbroken HOD or double top. Evidence: the LUNR and HOOD entries (MFE +0.14R and +0.52R, both -1R).
3. **Puts only on underlyings priced about $25 or less**, with |delta| ≥ 0.15 and premium ≤ $0.20. Evidence: 6 runs skipped puts as lotteries, and the SOFI chain check.

No change to the score threshold, stop method or targets. Two days of data point in opposite directions (trend vs chop).
