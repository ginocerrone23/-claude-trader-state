## Skipped-setup outcomes (49 rows appended to research/score-outcomes.csv)
Method: as on 11/16, with one refinement. The levels and score in force are those from the run that last evaluated the ticker+side, until the next run re-evaluates it. A trigger is the first completed 5-min close beyond the level in a bar starting at or after that run's time (rounded down to 5 min) and before 13:30 CT. Entry = that bar's close; outcomes run to the 14:40 bar close (the 2:45pm price); a bar touching both levels counts as a stop. When the run wrote "X (Y on trigger)" and the setup triggered, the recorded conditional score Y is used (the trader's own number, not a hindsight re-score). Otherwise it's the highest recorded score. Puts are graded on the underlying.
