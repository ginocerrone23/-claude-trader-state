**Verdict: REWEIGHT, don't rebuild, and don't scrap the score.**
- What the score gets right: the 7+ bucket is the best in both cuts (+0.44R all, +0.66R puts), and the room-to-resistance, chase and late-trigger rules removed real losers.
- What it gets wrong: (a) **the market-alignment point (+1) is far too small.** Side-versus-tape is the single biggest driver (puts +0.39R vs longs -0.43R), yet it moves the score by only 1. (b) **The rel-vol +2 is too big a gate for liquid large caps in a broad selloff**: the best first-hour puts this week sat at 1–1.7x and capped at 7. (c) The late-trigger -1 fits longs (-0.41R after 10:30) better than puts (+0.16R).
- **Proposed reweight (for Gino's approval; not applied):** market alignment +1 → **+2** (side matches the tape: SPY below VWAP and its prior close for puts, above both for longs); **longs against the tape -2** (replaces the separate RS-only watchlist rule); keep rel vol +2 for > 2x and add **+1 for 1.3–2x**; the late-trigger -1 stays for longs and becomes **-1 for puts only after 12:00 CT**. The max score becomes 10, so the floors would need re-reading against it. That's why I'm proposing it rather than applying it.

**Recommended floors (Gino's call; not changed):**
- **Stocks/ETFs/crypto: keep 7.** No long bucket at any score was profitable, so nothing argues for lowering it.
- **Options: keep 8 under the current scoring, OR adopt 7 for puts only when the trigger fires before 10:30 CT with the tape condition met** (SPY below VWAP and its prior close). Evidence: puts at score ≥ 7 +0.66R (n = 19, 47% target); puts at 6 +0.57R (n = 30); first-hour puts at ≥ 6 +0.74R (n = 36). Against it: n is small, and the evidence comes from one down week (excluding 11/20, puts at ≥ 7 were +0.57R on n = 7). **My recommendation is the conditional 7 for regime-aligned first-hour puts, re-evaluated after 2 more weeks.** If you prefer the reweight above, keep 8 and let the new weights lift aligned puts to 8.
